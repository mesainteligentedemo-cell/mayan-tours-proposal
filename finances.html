4. Recomendaciones Operacionales

Mes 1-3 (Foundation):

  • Usar LEAN stack ($3.1k/mes). No hay presión de escala.
  • Focus: Product-market fit, operator onboarding, brand credibility.
  • Marketing: Organic (founder, blog, influencer partnerships) + $10-20k PPC/mes.
  • Bots: 1 WhatsApp bot (MVP), email sequences, no video gen yet.

Mes 4-6 (Growth):

  • Move to GROWTH stack ($9k/mes) when revenue supports it (M4 target: $459k revenue).
  • Add: ElevenLabs for VO, Telegram + Email bots, n8n Cloud for complex workflows.
  • Increase marketing to $45-100k/mes. Double down on winners from M1-3.
  • Hiring: Consider first contractor ($2-3k/mes) for customer success.

Mes 7-12 (Scale):

  • Add video gen (Higgsfield, Kling, Runway) for marketing content. Budget: $100-150k.
  • Upgrade to HubSpot Pro ($750/mes) for marketing automation + lead scoring.
  • Add Mixpanel Pro ($499/mes) for deeper analytics. Optimize CAC.
  • Hire: Sales person ($2.5k), Content manager ($1.5k), Ops ($1.5k) = $5.5k extra.

5. Cuándo Escalar a Año 2

Si Revenue < $100k/mes en M12 Mantén LEAN stack. Enfoca en PMF antes de gastar.
Si Revenue $100-300k/mes en M12 Híbrido: LEAN infra + GROWTH marketing. Contrata 2-3 personas.
Si Revenue > $300k/mes en M12 Full GROWTH stack + 5-8 person team. Prepare for Series A o venture.

6. Riesgos Identificados

  • Operator churn: If 10% of tours are cancelled, revenue drops 10%. Mitigation: SLA guarantees, insurance.
  • Seasonality: Tours peak Dec-Apr (winter tourists), drop May-Nov. Model assumes smooth growth; reality will have waves.
  • Competition: Existing OTAs (Viator, GetYourGuide) may enter market. Differentiate on trust + bots.
  • Marketing CAC: If Facebook/Google raise prices, CAC goes 3x. Diversify to organic + referrals.
  • API costs: OpenAI/Claude may increase pricing. Lock in volume discounts early, or build own models.
